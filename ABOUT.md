# About OpenZone Taurians

## What is it?

OpenZone Taurians is a **desktop AI assistant** that integrates AI models directly with your desktop environment. Instead of switching between a browser, a chat window, and your tools, OpenZone brings the AI to where your work actually happens — your machine.

## The Problem

AI assistants today mostly live in browser tabs, disconnected from your files, apps, and system. Getting them to actually *do* work on your behalf is clunky.

## The Solution

By building on [Tauri](https://tauri.app/), OpenZone Taurians runs as a true native application with:

- Direct, secure access to the operating system
- A small, fast footprint (Rust backend)
- The ability to integrate AI models with real desktop tasks

## Vision

A desktop companion that understands context, automates repetitive work, and helps you finish tasks faster — while keeping you in control of your data and privacy.
